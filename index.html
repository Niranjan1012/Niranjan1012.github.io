About

Engineering with Precision

I completed my Master of Applied Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Ontario Tech University, where my thesis focused on wireless charging, power converter design & development, hybrid compensation networks, and closed-loop control system design.

Currently at Wireless PnC Inc., I lead end-to-end development of WPT systems for EVs, e-bikes, and e-scooters — spanning boost PFC rectifiers, full-bridge SiC inverters, PCB layout in KiCad, and system-level fault diagnosis and validation.

I thrive at the intersection of simulation and hardware: building a model in PSIM, validating it on the bench, and closing the loop until the waveform matches theory.
